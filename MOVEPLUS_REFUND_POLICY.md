
> # MOVEPLUS REFUND & REDEMPTION POLICY
> 
> 
> **Last Updated: May 9, 2026**
> At Move+, we want to ensure a fair ecosystem for our "Move-to-Earn" community. Because our rewards are earned through physical effort ("Sweat Equity"), our policy is structured as follows:
> ### 1. ENERGY & ENR (DIGITAL UTILITY)
> 
> 
> * **No Cash Value:** Energy and ENR are earned loyalty points and have no monetary value. They cannot be "refunded" for cash, PHP, or any other fiat currency.
> * **Irreversible Actions:** Once Energy is converted to ENR or used for a "Repair" or "Upgrade," the action is permanent and cannot be reversed.
> 
> 
> ### 2. PHYSICAL GOODS (MARKETPLACE)
> 
> 
> In compliance with the **Consumer Act of the Philippines**, we offer protections for physical items claimed via the Marketplace:
> * **Defective Items:** If you receive an item that is damaged or defective, you must notify us at `kit.moveplus@gmail.com` within 48 hours of delivery.
> * **Resolution:** For verified defective items, Move+ will either:
> 1. Ship a replacement item at no extra Energy cost.
> 2. If the item is out of stock, refund the exact amount of **Energy** used for the claim back to your account.
> 
> 
> * **Change of Mind:** We do not offer refunds or Energy reversals for "change of mind" once a physical item has been shipped.
> 
> 
> ### 3. ACCOUNT SUSPENSION
> 
> 
> * **Fraud/Cheating:** If an account is suspended for violating our **Anti-Cheat Policy** (e.g., GPS spoofing), all remaining Energy and ENR balances are voided and are non-refundable.
> 
> 
> ### 4. CONTACT
> 
> 
> For all support and redemption inquiries, please contact: `moveplus@gmail.com`.
