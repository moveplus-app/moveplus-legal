

## MOVE+ PRIVACY POLICY 

**Last Updated: May 2026**

Move+ is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

---

### 1. DATA WE COLLECT

To provide our activity-based features, we collect the following types of data:

**Fitness & Activity Data**
We access your device’s motion sensors and GPS to track distance, steps, and activity type (Walking, Running, Cycling).

**Location Data**
We collect precise location data during activity sessions to verify movement and maintain accuracy.

**Account Information**
We collect basic account details such as your email address or login credentials.

**Optional External Account Data**
If you choose to connect an external account (such as a digital wallet), we may collect a public identifier associated with that account.
We do not collect or store private keys, passwords, or sensitive credentials.

**Shipping Information**
If you redeem physical rewards, we collect your name, phone number, and delivery address.

---

### 2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

We use your data for the following purposes:

* **Activity Tracking & Rewards**
  To calculate and assign in-app rewards based on verified physical activity.

* **App Functionality**
  To display your activity history, digital gear, and progression.

* **Reward Fulfillment**
  To process and deliver physical rewards when redeemed.

* **Security & Fair Use**
  To detect and prevent abuse such as location spoofing or fraudulent activity.

---

### 3. DATA SHARING & THIRD PARTIES

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share data only in the following cases:

* **Service Providers**
  With trusted partners such as delivery providers to fulfill physical rewards.

* **External Systems**
  If you choose to connect an external account, certain data may be associated with that system.
  These systems operate independently and may have their own privacy practices.

---

### 4. DIGITAL REWARDS & VIRTUAL ASSETS

Move+ uses a virtual reward system (“Energy” and “ENR”) to support in-app features such as activity tracking, gear progression, and rewards.

These virtual rewards:

* Have no real-world monetary value
* Cannot be withdrawn, transferred, or exchanged
* Are used solely within the Move+ application
* Do not represent currency, financial assets, or investment products

If you choose to link an external account, certain digital assets may be associated with that account through third-party systems. Move+ does not control or guarantee the availability of such external systems.

---

### 5. DATA RETENTION & DELETION

* **Retention**
  We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services.

* **Deletion**
  You may request account deletion at any time through the app. Personal data will be removed within 30 days, except where required for legal or operational purposes.

---

### 6. YOUR PRIVACY CHOICES

* **Location Permissions**
  You can enable or disable location tracking through your device settings.

* **External Account Linking**
  You may connect or disconnect external accounts at any time.

---

### 7. SECURITY

We use industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit and secure data handling practices.

---

### 8. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

Move+ is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

---

### 9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the “Last Updated” date.
